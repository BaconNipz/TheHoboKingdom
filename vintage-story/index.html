Vintage Story

A shared record of the world, role split, early progression, exact mod stack, server setup, and the problems that need fixing before the next attempt.

Multiplayer trial Recorded on 1.22.3 Modded Updated 27 August 2026

The role split

Smith

Handles copper, tools, moulds, metalworking progression, and the jobs that unlock stronger equipment for the whole group.

Farmer

Builds the dependable food supply, expands crops, manages storage, and keeps the group from living entirely on whatever can be found nearby.

Blackguard

Handles dangerous exploration, defence, hunting, and the combat work that lets the other roles keep developing the settlement.

Latest recorded checkpoint

The first trial reached the pottery age in roughly an hour and a half. A cellar and storage area were established, with four crocks, four cooking pots, four bowls, fired pick and hammer moulds, and two crucibles recorded. Three storage vessels remained an early target.

Food was expected to last about eight days. Copper was the immediate bottleneck, with the group around halfway to the forty nuggets needed for the first proper tool cycle and panning continuing at night.

Mod record

The complete manifest still needs to be frozen before it can be treated as a stable pack. Mods already important to the record include Wild Farming Revival, Primitive Survival, A Culinary Artillery, Ancient Tools, VSImGui, Overhaul Lib, JSON Patches Lib, and the shader stack.

Dependency note: VSImGui 1.2.7 was identified as a required dependency during the earlier setup. Ancient Tools and A Culinary Artillery also appeared in crash reports and need exact version checks before the next public manifest.

Before the next world

  3. Record confirmed crash fixes separately from guesses and abandoned attempts.
  4. Keep a shared progression checklist for food security, copper, tools, storage, and the next technological jump.
  5. Package the tested mod set only after every player can join the same world.
