# Progress Edition 27 - Evidence and Nation-Dossier Release

**Release date:** 28 August 2026  
**Base game:** Dominions 6.36  
**Official baseline:** revision-2 manual and official changes through 6.36

## Scope

Edition 27 closes the veteran Hit Point version conflict, expands Book VII with full unmodded Middle Age Marignon and Middle Age Pyrène dossiers, and publishes both nation chapters as compact standalone field guides. It also carries forward the Edition 26 high-priority evidence audit and the Edition 25 base-object register.

## New nation dossiers

Book VII now contains release-ready chapters for:

- Middle Age Marignon, Fiery Justice;
- Middle Age Pyrène, Time of the Akelarre.

Each dossier includes a one-page command brief, roster and path evidence, opening priorities, research response trees, battlefield packages, Pretender families, matchup planning, and a nation-specific verification ledger. Exact random paths use the pinned Inspector export; roster and national descriptions use the official revision-2 manual; strategic recommendations are labelled as doctrine rather than rules.

## Split reader set

The 29 August companion release keeps the complete omnibus and adds four smaller entry points:

- **Core Rules and Strategy:** the Reader's Guide and Foundation Books I-VI;
- **Middle Age Nation Compendium, Volume I:** the complete Arcoscephale, Marignon, and Pyrène dossiers from Book VII;
- **Technical and Modding Reference:** Foundation Books VIII-XIV;
- **Turn and Economy Quick Reference:** the compact field reference as its own file.

The split readers retain the original book and section names. Links to material outside a particular volume remain readable text instead of becoming broken PDF destinations.
