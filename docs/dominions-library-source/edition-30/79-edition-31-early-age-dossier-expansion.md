# Edition 31 Early Age dossier expansion

**Working date:** 14 September 2026
**Live rules baseline:** Dominions 6.37
**Structured source:** Dominions 6 Data Inspector 6.35, commit `cfac4311bc0b58053b8dead7bffbc036ba9bd5dc`

## Result

Book VII now contains a complete source-backed dossier for all 35 unmodded Early Age nations. Together with Edition 30's 37 Middle Age chapters, the public dossier index now covers 72 of the game's 102 official nations.

The rebuilt website contains sixteen documents, 4,591 indexed sections, and 393,838 words. Thirty-five standalone Early Age field readers accompany the web chapters.

## Method

Each chapter keeps the same split used by the Middle Age collection:

- direct roster, site, path, spell, item, and hero records;
- capacity derived from those records;
- strategy presented as bounded doctrine;
- runtime-dependent behaviour left open where no controlled result exists.

The revision-2 official manual governs player-facing descriptions. The 6.35 Inspector snapshot supplies exact structured fields and is not relabelled as live 6.37 data. Generic 6.37 statistic corrections are not attached to unnamed objects.

## Remaining boundary

Expansion counts, live random displays, formations, scripts, freespawn and reanimation composition, special dominion timing, transformations, mounts, summons, item-price stacking, hero timing, stealth detection, sailing, habitat transitions, events, and battle outcomes remain unresolved where the available sources do not settle them. Hands-on runtime testing remains parked.

Late Age dossier production is the next library expansion stage.
