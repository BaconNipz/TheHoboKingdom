# TheHoboKingdom writing voice

This is the standard for public website copy. It does not apply to the Dominions Knowledge Library books or other quoted source material.

## Point of view

- Use **I** when explaining why I made a project, what I am working on, or what I plan to do next.
- Use **we** and **our** for shared worlds, campaigns, packs, and table decisions.
- Keep rules, reference entries, and tool instructions neutral and factual.
- Never call a visitor “the user” in public copy. Name the player, reader, GM, or group when that distinction matters.

## How it should sound

- Write plainly and get to the point.
- Prefer short headings and ordinary words.
- Give the exact version, date, source, test state, or limitation when it matters.
- Say what a tool records and what it does not calculate.
- State uncertainty directly: “I have not tested this yet” is better than vague caution.
- Keep status, next work, and finished releases separate.

## Phrases to avoid

- Corporate or promotional language such as “seamless”, “robust”, “empower”, and “streamline”.
- Stock claims such as “a comprehensive solution”, “designed to”, or “built around”.
- Repeated constructions such as “X without Y”, especially when two direct sentences would be clearer.
- Empty praise such as “powerful”, “meaningful”, “valuable”, or “useful” unless the sentence explains why.
- Neat three-part summaries added only to make a paragraph sound finished.
- Claims that a tool “silently” does or does not decide something. Say exactly what it calculates, stores, or leaves to the player.

## Reference and evidence copy

- Mark material as Official rule, Tested result, Strategy, Modded, Open research, or Playtest where the distinction matters.
- Do not turn a recommendation into a rule.
- Do not fill gaps with an invented number, balance claim, Pretender, research route, or expected result.
- Keep sourced facts separate from my interpretation and from work that still needs testing.
