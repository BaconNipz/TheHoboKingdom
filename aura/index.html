Projects/AURA

Adaptive Utility for Real-world Advancement

A real-life progression system that behaves like a game.

AURA turns goals, habits, useful skills, and long-term improvement into quests, levels, rewards, titles, and a changing character build.

Working prototype Version 2 tested Mobile application Updated 27 August 2026

Current state: version 2 is running without a known functional problem. The next release is meant to be a substantial development step, including clearer low-contrast status text and deeper systems rather than a small cosmetic patch.

What AURA is

AURA is part companion and part game system, though the presentation leans strongly toward the game side. It does not lock a person into one route. It learns from goals and behaviour, offers useful work as quests, and gradually reshapes the experience around what is actually happening.

The core systems

Attributes and skills

Individual skills feed into ten broader attributes: Vigor, Cognition, Presence, Resolve, Capability, Vocation, Stewardship, Fortitude, Discovery, and Vitality. Attribute levels then contribute to the overall player level.

Classes and jobs

After onboarding, AURA offers several real-world archetypes matched to the stated goals. One is chosen as the active direction, but it never locks content and can be changed without a penalty.

Quests

Most quests are created dynamically from goals and observed behaviour. A smaller curated library and manual user-created quests remain available. Self-selected quests carry slightly greater rewards and proportionally greater penalties.

Gold and titles

Gold acts as the main spendable reward. Titles recognise meaningful patterns and achievements, such as consistently improving a wake-up time, rather than being handed out for trivial actions.

Rewards and consequences

Rewards and penalties are intended to carry comparable weight. The system should make success feel worthwhile without turning a missed task into punishment severe enough to make the app easier to abandon.

Hidden mastery

Visible skill levels stop at 100. Progress continues behind the scenes and can unlock titles, recognition, rewards, unusual quests, and mastery benefits without making the visible numbers meaningless.

Next development pass

  4. Develop classes, Gold, titles, and Level 100 mastery into working systems.
  5. Prepare a reliable build and installation route once the application is ready for a first full release.

What will be public

Design explanations, screenshots, release notes, installation instructions, and stable application builds can be published here. Personal goals, behavioural records, private quest history, and any data produced by someone using AURA do not belong on the public website.
