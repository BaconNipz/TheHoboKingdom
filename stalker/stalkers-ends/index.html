Working fiction collection

Stalkers' Ends

Every entry follows a different stalker. Some are taken by anomalies, some by mutants, some by other people, and some by the choices that brought them into the Zone.

In developmentShort fictionCharacter memorialsWorking title

The shape of the collection

The entries are linked by the Zone rather than one continuing protagonist. A page can follow a veteran, a bandit, a scientist, a frightened newcomer, or someone who barely receives a name before the place closes around them.

The deaths should not become a catalogue of gore. The useful part is the person beforehand: what they believed, what they ignored, what they carried, and the last small decision that made the end unavoidable.

Planned kinds of ending

Anomalies

Deaths caused by a place that appeared empty until it was already too late.

Mutants

Encounters where poor preparation, bad terrain, panic, or simple bad luck matters as much as the creature.

Other stalkers

Betrayals, mistaken identity, faction grudges, robbery, and the ordinary violence people carry into the Zone.

The stalker themself

Greed, pride, exhaustion, addiction, obsession, or one last attempt to recover something that should have been left behind.

Publishing rule

Only finished entries will appear in the public reading order. Draft fragments, abandoned versions, and research notes stay outside the collection so the published pages still feel like a book rather than a workshop folder.
