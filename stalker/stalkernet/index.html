StalkerNet

StalkerNet is my mobile-friendly S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly PDA for messages, jobs, maps, field records, and a shared in-universe archive.

Stable baseline v4.3.6 Firebase Mobile-first Updated 27 August 2026

Recovery point: StalkerNet_Cloud_Sync_Status_v4_3_6.zip is the latest build I have confirmed as working. I will keep it untouched until a newer build passes the same checks.

Project purpose

I want StalkerNet to feel like a Zone PDA while still being a useful mobile application. It combines player messages with jobs, identification, maps, field references, settings, and cloud-backed records.

Working feature areas

  • Comms: chat, search and filters, lightweight drafts, moderation tools, blocking, deletion, and online or last-seen state.
  • Map: world and sub-zone maps, default locations, custom markers, filters, descriptions, and cloud-saved markers.
  • Archive: factions, mutants, anomalies, medicine, public dossiers, and searchable reference material.
  • Jobs: job listings with filter and sort behaviour.
  • ID: personal profile, faction patch, rank, battery, and signal presentation.
  • Settings: display preferences, audio cues, install and cache tools, backup and restore, error reporting, and cloud-sync status.
