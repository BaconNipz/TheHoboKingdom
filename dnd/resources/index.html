Guides/Dungeons & Dragons/Player resources

Public campaign material

D&D player resources

A permanent home for material that can be handed to the table without exposing the notes meant to stay behind the screen.

Collections

Character journals

Public backstories, completed character events, boons, scars, changing loyalties, and the parts of a character sheet worth remembering between sessions.

Quick references

Compact rules or ability reminders written for the exact character or adventure using them.

Player handouts

Letters, notices, maps, item records, and other material intended to exist in front of the players.

Table-ready pieces

Finished scenes, encounters, NPCs, and consequences that can be placed into a campaign without needing another writing pass first.

Publishing rule

A resource is public only when every hidden answer, future consequence, private player detail, and unrevealed GM note has been removed. A blank template can be public immediately; a live campaign record may need to wait until the relevant events are over.
