The story

A Week at a Time follows Eli, a nineteen-year-old who becomes the sole guardian of his five-year-old sister, Mara, after a catastrophic outbreak of a rapidly progressing, rabies-like infection destroys their family and brings civilisation to its knees.

With nowhere to call home and nobody he can bring himself to trust, Eli leads his little sister through abandoned neighbourhoods, deteriorating towns, and the remnants of a collapsing society.

As the weeks pass, the siblings gradually learn to survive the infected, whose damaged brains drive them into unpredictable fits of violence while their bodies slowly waste away. But the greatest threat to Eli isn't necessarily the infected. It's the overwhelming grief, exhaustion, guilt, and growing hopelessness that threaten to consume him.

Despite his deteriorating mental state, Eli remains determined to give Mara something resembling a childhood, even as he loses more and more of himself in the process.

When the siblings eventually encounter an unusually intelligent and physically imposing infected that begins hunting them, everything they've learned about survival is put to the test.

At its heart, A Week at a Time is a story about grief, brotherhood, sacrifice, and the lengths someone will go to protect the last person they have left.
