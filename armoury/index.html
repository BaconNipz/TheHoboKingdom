What each article should contain

  • The finished narration or transcript in readable form.
  • A short specification table and development timeline.
  • The weapon's actual use, strengths, weaknesses, and common misconceptions.
  • Sources and further reading close to the claims they support.
  • Credits and licence details for every photograph, diagram, and piece of footage used.
  • A corrections section so later discoveries do not require quietly rewriting history.

First planned piece

The Sten: the first companion article will be published when the narration, footage list, source check, and finished video are all ready. Until then, this remains a project record rather than an empty article dressed up as a release.
