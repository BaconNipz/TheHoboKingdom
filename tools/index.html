Record safety

Private does not have to mean fragile.

Saved state stays on the current device. The new record tools can also export a dated JSON recovery file and restore it later on another browser.

01

Local first

Working data is stored in the browser and is never submitted to TheHoboKingdom.

02

Recoverable

Exported JSON preserves the structured record instead of reducing everything to a screenshot.

03

Non-prescriptive

The tools organise chosen facts and plans. They do not silently score a strategy or certify a mod stack.
