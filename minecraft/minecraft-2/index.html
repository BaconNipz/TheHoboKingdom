Minecraft 2

A large Forge pack intended to feel like a bigger continuation of ordinary Minecraft rather than a pile of unrelated systems.

Building and testingForgeVanilla-friendly directionVersion not frozen

Release boundary: the final Minecraft version, Forge version, mod list, dependency list, recipe set, and profile export must be tested together. Until then, this page records the design rather than claiming there is a stable pack to download.

Design rules

  • Ordinary Minecraft remains recognisable from the first night to the late game.
  • New systems should strengthen the main progression rather than create several disconnected games inside one pack.
  • Recipes should be understandable from the ingredients and use familiar visual logic.
  • Food, farming, fishing, wildlife, exploration, storage, and combat should support each other.
  • Difficulty should come from decisions and preparation, not unexplained inconvenience.

Planned progression

Early game

Carry what can be made

Bundles become the foundation of a pouch and backpack line instead of backpacks appearing as an isolated convenience item.

Settlement

Food becomes a system

Raw ingredients give less benefit while proper meals, farming, fishing, and stored food become worthwhile parts of survival.

Exploration

The world earns the travel

Tectonic, Terralith, structures, wildlife, and useful discoveries are meant to reward leaving the first base without breaking the vanilla shape of the world.

Late game

A larger step beyond vanilla

The finished pack should provide a meaningful new stretch of progression after the ordinary milestones rather than ending as soon as familiar equipment is obtained.

Release checklist

  1. Freeze Minecraft, Forge, and every mod version.
  2. Record required libraries and confirm the pack launches from a clean profile.
  3. Test new-world generation and travel far enough to catch obvious worldgen conflicts.
  4. Test every custom recipe in survival and check that recipe viewers display it correctly.
  5. Have another player install from the written instructions without relying on missing verbal steps.
  6. Export the CurseForge profile, version it, and record known limitations before publishing.
