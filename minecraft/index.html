Minecraft

Large friend-group modpacks that extend Minecraft's progression while trying to remain coherent, maintainable, and recognisably vanilla.

Active Forge CurseForge Modpacks

Current direction

The active pack is being assembled for a group of friends using standard Forge. The goal is a large progression-focused pack that builds naturally on Minecraft instead of feeling like an unrelated collection of mods.

Planned progression changes

  • Bundles can become the foundation of a new pouch and backpack progression.
  • Raw ingredients provide less benefit so complex meals have a clear purpose.
  • Farming, fishing, wildlife, storage, and exploration feed into the same progression.
  • Custom recipes remain understandable and visually consistent with vanilla items.

Current project state

Building and testing: the final Minecraft version, mod list, recipe changes, and distribution package should be recorded together once the core set is stable.
