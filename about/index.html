About the site

A practical archive for projects that refuse to stay small.

TheHoboKingdom began as a place to keep game notes and has grown into a home for guides, mods, applications, campaign material, and shared projects.

What belongs here

The site focuses on material that remains useful beyond a single session: working guides, stable project records, modpack instructions, printable handouts, and the history behind ongoing builds.

How material is organised

Projects are grouped by game. Guides have stable addresses and clear update dates. Downloads are kept separate from drafts so an unfinished experiment cannot be mistaken for a reliable release.

Built in the open

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Its source and history are available in the TheHoboKingdom repository.
