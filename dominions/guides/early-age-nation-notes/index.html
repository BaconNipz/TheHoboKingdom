Nation dossiers

Arcoscephale — Golden Age of Man

Troops and commanders

  • Militia, light infantry, and peltasts: inexpensive line holders and flankers with javelins and shields.
  • Charioteers and elephants: early expansion tools whose impact depends heavily on the opposing formation.
  • Heart Companions: capital-only sacred cavalry intended to provide a high-morale striking force.
  • Mystics and philosophers: the foundation of flexible magic access and efficient research.
  • Oreads and Hiereiai: stealthy magical and priestly support.

Working assessment

Diverse elemental access and inexpensive research create a flexible magical game. Early expansion can lean on chariots, elephants, or sacred cavalry while the research engine builds toward communions and broader battlefield support.

Mekone — Brazen Giants

Troops and commanders

  • Helotes: cheap bodies for screening, arrows, and siege work.
  • Gigante troops: elite giant formations that win direct fights but make every loss expensive.
  • Archons, polemarchs, and cyclopes: leadership, battlefield magic, crafting, and thug potential.
  • Kryptes: assassination pressure tied to Mekone's internal control machinery.

Working assessment

Mekone combines high-quality giants with expendable helot support. Recruitment limits and unrest make clean expansion and careful loss management more important than winning costly battles.

Pangaea — Revels of the Wild

Troops and commanders

  • Satyrs and centaurs: line troops, archers, and fast flank pressure.
  • Maenads: free-spawning berserk bodies that add numbers and siege strength.
  • Minotaurs and White Centaurs: trampling shock troops and sacred cavalry.
  • Pans and dryads: strong nature support, stealth, and possible access to blood magic.

Working assessment

Numbers, stealth, mobility, and nature buffs allow Pangaea to pressure wide areas. The precise research plan should be built around the recruited Pan paths and whether blood hunting is part of the game plan.

Ermor — Legions of the Old Faith

Troops and commanders

  • Hastati, principes, and triarii: disciplined legionary formations with distinct roles.
  • Equites and gladiators: mobile flankers and disposable high-damage troops.
  • Augurs and Augur Elders: astral and death researchers that support communion play.
  • Pontifices and legion commanders: priestly support and disciplined army leadership.

Working assessment

Reliable infantry and astral-death mages provide a conventional expansion game that can develop into communion-backed battlefield control.
