Working guide

Middle Age research builds

Starter research lines that reach common breakpoints quickly. Exact priorities still depend on the nation, map, neighbours, and independent strength.

Working draft Dominions 6 Middle Age Preserved 23 July 2026

Scope: these are broad starting points rather than nation-specific prescriptions. Spell availability and item names should be checked against the active game version and mod list before this draft is treated as final.

Quick research lines

Generalist Construction 2 → Alteration 3 → Evocation 4 → Construction 4 Safe baseline
Bless pressure Alteration 3 → Enchantment 3 → Conjuration 3 Early buffs
Mage core Construction 4 → Evocation 5 → Alteration 4 Scaling

Common breakpoints

  • Construction 2: basic equipment and early utility items.
  • Alteration 3: defensive buffs such as Body Ethereal, Luck, and Protection where paths allow them.
  • Evocation 4–5: the first meaningful area-damage options for several common paths.
  • Construction 4: stronger equipment and several important research or path-boosting options.

By broad archetype

Elf and stealth nations

Alteration 3 → Enchantment 3 → Conjuration 3 → Evocation 4

The early focus is defensive magic, utility, and fights chosen on favourable terms before adding wider battlefield pressure.

Heavy infantry lines

Alteration 3 → Evocation 4 → Construction 4

Durable troops benefit from early defensive and strength support, followed by area damage and more dependable equipment.

Communion nations

Construction 4 → Alteration 4 → Evocation 5

The goal is to establish the support needed for reliable communions before scaling into stronger battlefield magic.

Revision priorities
