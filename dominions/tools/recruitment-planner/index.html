Upkeep limits

Ordinary upkeep is gold cost divided by 15 per month. Sacred or Slave units use 30; the Sacred-and-Slave divisor of 60 is community-tested under 6.33 and remains current through 6.36 by patch review. Mounted cards can contain separate rider and mount upkeep bases, and some summoned units are exceptions. Use the detailed unit interface when exact upkeep is available.
