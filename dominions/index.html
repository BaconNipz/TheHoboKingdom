Official references

The nation catalogue and game-setup guide are based on Illwinter's current Dominions 6 manual. The technical side of the project will use the official modding, event, and map-making manuals where those subjects apply.

Illwinter documentation Main manual, modding manuals, map tools, and introductory material. ↗

Active project: computer diplomacy overhaul

The aim is to make computer-controlled nations react more clearly to the state of a campaign. Expansion, throne claims, dominion pressure, raids, and prolonged wars could produce warnings, grudges, defensive agreements, and eventually coalitions.

Status: concept and feasibility work. Development is paused while the limits of event-driven diplomacy and possible formation additions are investigated.
