Dominions 6 Knowledge Library

{{ site.data.dominions_library.edition_label }} has a permanent website edition. Its sixteen books and field references cover turn structure, economy, Pretenders, battle, magic, strategy, nations, modding, player operations, unit abilities, base-game objects, official patch history, and the command lexicon.

Current baseline: Dominions {{ site.data.dominions_library.game_version }}, Dominions Enhanced {{ site.data.dominions_library.de_version }}, and Divinitus {{ site.data.dominions_library.divinitus_version }}. Modded material remains clearly separated from ordinary base-game procedure.

How the material is labelled

Official rule Tested result Strategy Modded Open research

Official statements, strategic recommendations, modded material, controlled tests, and unresolved questions are kept visibly separate. A useful recommendation should never be mistaken for a rule printed by Illwinter.

Official references

The nation catalogue and game-setup guide are based on Illwinter's current Dominions 6 manual. The technical side of the project will use the official modding, event, and map-making manuals where those subjects apply.

Active project: computer diplomacy overhaul

The aim is to make computer-controlled nations react more clearly to the state of a campaign. Expansion, throne claims, dominion pressure, raids, and prolonged wars could produce warnings, grudges, defensive agreements, and eventually coalitions.

Status: concept and feasibility work. Development is paused while the limits of event-driven diplomacy and possible formation additions are investigated.
