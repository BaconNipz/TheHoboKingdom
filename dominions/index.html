Active project: AI diplomacy overhaul

The goal is to make multiplayer feel less like nations are fighting random opponents and more like every neighbour is watching what happens. Aggressive expansion, throne claims, dominion pressure, raids, and warmongering should produce warnings, grudges, defensive pacts, and eventually coalitions.

Status: concept and feasibility work. The project is currently paused while the limits of event-driven diplomacy and possible formation additions are investigated.

Next additions

  • A reusable nation-guide format covering expansion, research, magic access, and common threats.
  • A permanent page for the diplomacy-overhaul design and technical findings.
  • Campaign records for Dominions Enhanced and Divinitus games.
  • Pretender templates that clearly state their supported mods and game version.
