Dominions 6 Knowledge Library

{{ site.data.dominions_library.edition_label }} is available both as a website and as a PDF. Its sixteen books and references cover turn structure, economy, Pretenders, battle, magic, strategy, nations, modding, player operations, unit abilities, base-game objects, patch history, and commands.

Current baseline: Dominions {{ site.data.dominions_library.game_version }}, Dominions Enhanced {{ site.data.dominions_library.de_version }}, and Divinitus {{ site.data.dominions_library.divinitus_version }}. Anything that depends on a mod is labelled separately from the base game.

How the material is labelled

Official rule Tested result Strategy Modded Open research

The labels separate rules from strategy, mods, controlled tests, and open questions. If something is my recommendation, it should not read like a rule from Illwinter.

Active project: computer diplomacy overhaul

I want computer-controlled nations to react more convincingly to the state of a campaign. Expansion, Throne claims, dominion pressure, raids, and long wars could lead to warnings, grudges, defensive agreements, and coalitions.

Status: paused at the concept and feasibility stage while I work out the limits of event-driven diplomacy and possible formation additions.
